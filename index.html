I am a Professor in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign. I am interested in the interplay between geometry/topology and algorithms. Current research is mainly in structure-preserving discretizations of exterior calculus and differential geometry. Discretization = combinatorial version of calculus and differential geometry on graphs and simplicial complexes. An example of structure preservation is the first theorem in this paper on vector bundles. The structures are interesting in their own right and also useful in applications. Recently I have also started exploring machine learning and AI. For more details on active and past projects, please see my Research page.

Current funding and RA opportunity: Collaborative Research: Discretization of Exterior Calculus and Differential Geometry. National Science Foundation, Division of Mathematical Sciences.
