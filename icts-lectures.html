DEC lectures at ICTS-TIFR, November 2025

Recent (2025) introduction to Discrete Exterior Calculus (DEC), delivered November 3–14, 2025 in Bengaluru, India, as part of the Geometry, Mechanics and the Physics of Growth program at the International Centre for Theoretical Sciences (ICTS), Tata Institute of Fundamental Research.

Lecture 1: Introduction to Discrete Exterior Calculus (DEC)

Watch on YouTube →
Motivation for discrete exterior calculus (DEC); de Rham complex; Simplicial complexes; Discrete differential forms; Discrete exterior derivative; Discrete wedge product; Leibniz (product) rule; Simplicial maps and naturality

Lecture 2: Metric and other operators in DEC

Watch on YouTube →
Dual cell complex; Discrete Hodge star; Codifferential; Hodge-Laplace problems; Relationship with finite element exterior calculus and Whitney forms; Discrete contraction and Lie derivative

Lecture 3: Vector bundle valued DEC and elasticity

Watch on YouTube →
Exterior covariant derivative; Discrete vector bundles with connection; Discrete exterior covariant derivative; Discrete curvature; Discrete Bianchi identity. If there is time left then: Bernstein-Gelfand-Gelfand construction with application to elasticity complex
